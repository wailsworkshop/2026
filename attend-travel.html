Paris

Paris, the capital of France, is one of the world's most iconic cities, a place where history, culture, and intellectual life converge. Home to world-renowned museums, grand boulevards, and a vibrant academic scene, Paris offers an unparalleled setting for an international workshop. In December, the city takes on a special charm, with its illuminated streets and festive atmosphere inviting visitors to explore its many neighbourhoods, from the Latin Quarter to Montmartre.

Sorbonne Université Sciences et Ingénierie

WAILS 2026 will be hosted at Sorbonne Université Sciences et Ingénierie, one of France's leading research universities and a founding member of the Sorbonne family of institutions. Located in the heart of Paris on the historic Campus Pierre et Marie Curie, the Faculty of Sciences brings together researchers from mathematics, physics, chemistry, biology, computer science, and engineering. Its long tradition of scientific excellence, rooted in the legacy of Marie Curie, Pierre Curie, and many other pioneers, makes it an inspiring venue for a workshop at the intersection of artificial intelligence and education.

Getting to Paris

By Plane

Paris is served by two main international airports. Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG) is the largest and best connected, with direct flights from most major cities worldwide. Orly Airport (ORY) serves mainly European and domestic destinations. Both airports are well connected to the city centre by public transport.

From the Airport to the City

From CDG, take the RER B train to central Paris (approximately 35 minutes), then change to Metro M7 at the station Châtelet–Les Halles to reach Jussieu.
From Orly, take the Metro M14, then change to Metro M7 at the station Maison Blanche and exit at Jussieu.

Getting to the Campus

The campus Pierre et Marie Curie is located at 4 place Jussieu, 75005 Paris, in the 5th arrondissement. It is easily accessible by public transport:

  • Metro: Line 7 or Line 10, station Jussieu (direct access to the campus)
  • Bus: Lines 67, 86, 87, 89 — stop Jussieu
  • RER: Line C, station Gare d'Austerlitz (10 minutes on foot)

By Train

SNCF services connect Brussels, Amsterdam, Lyon, Marseille, and many other destinations. All major Paris train stations (Gare du Nord, Gare de Lyon, Gare Montparnasse) are well connected to the campus by metro.

Accommodation

The campus is surrounded by a wide range of accommodation options in the 5th and 6th arrondissements (Latin Quarter), within walking distance or a short metro ride. We recommend booking early, as Paris can be busy in December. A list of suggested hotels will be published here soon.
