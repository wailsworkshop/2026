Accessibility & Inclusion

WAILS 2026 is about the responsible and inclusive use of AI in education, and we want the workshop itself to reflect these values. We are committed to making it possible for everyone to take part fully and comfortably.

← Back to Home

Participation for Everyone

To participate comfortably in the workshop we have considered a wide range of needs, including step-free access and space for wheelchairs, accessible seating and reserved seats, accessible toilets, documents in accessible digital formats, live captions or sign-language interpretation, hearing support, and the presence of a support person or an assistance animal. We are also attentive to needs that are less visible: participants who would benefit from a quiet space, sensory adjustments, or additional breaks during the sessions can let us know, and we will do our best to make this possible.

You do not need to explain your situation in detail, and you can always choose to discuss your needs with us privately.

Meals and Dietary Requirements

Sharing a meal is part of the workshop experience, and everyone should be able to enjoy it. Dietary requirements, allergies, intolerances, and preferences, are taken into consideration so that the catering can offer suitable options, including gluten-free, lactose-free, vegetarian, vegan, halal, kosher meals, food allergy or intolerance.

Names and Pronouns

We want every participant to be addressed as they wish. Your badge can show the name you prefer to use, even if it differs from the name on your registration, and you can add your pronouns if you would like to. Both are entirely optional.

Confidentiality

Information about accessibility needs, dietary requirements, and personal identity is sensitive. Everything you share with us is handled confidentially and is accessible only to the organisers who need it to provide the support you have requested. Providing this information is always voluntary.

Code of Conduct

WAILS 2026 is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all participants, whatever their background, identity, or abilities. If you experience or witness behaviour that makes you feel uncomfortable or unsafe, you can contact our designated Code of Conduct contact person, Camille Miele. All reports will be treated confidentially.
