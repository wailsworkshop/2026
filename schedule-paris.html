The Festive Season

Christmas illuminations, markets, and seasonal events typically begin in late November and continue through the end of the year, transforming Paris into one of the most enchanting cities in the world during this period.

Illuminations

The Champs-Élysées, Place Vendôme, and Boulevard Haussmann are famous for their festive lights. Department stores such as Le Bon Marché and Galeries Lafayette unveil elaborate Christmas window displays, and the Eiffel Tower and Champ de Mars area feature seasonal light installations.

Christmas Markets

Numerous Christmas markets pop up across the city, from the Tuileries Garden to Saint-Germain-des-Prés and La Défense, offering local crafts, mulled wine, and seasonal treats — a great way to spend an evening between sessions.

Museums & Culture

Paris is home to world-renowned museums and cultural institutions, many within easy reach of the venue: the Louvre, Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou, and the Musée de Cluny (medieval art), among many others.
