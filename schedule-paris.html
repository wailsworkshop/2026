What's On in Paris

WAILS 2026 takes place in early December, right at the start of Paris's festive season. Planning to arrive a day or two early, or to stay on after the workshop? Here is what is on in the city during the week of 7–13 December 2026.

The Festive Season

The workshop days (9–11 December) are full, so the best moments to explore are the evenings, the days just before and after, and the weekend of 12–13 December. The items below are open or running throughout that week.

Illuminations & Shop Windows

The Champs-Élysées illuminations run from mid-November 2026 to early January 2027 and are switched on every evening from 5 pm to midnight. The department stores on Boulevard Haussmann and Le Bon Marché on the Left Bank are also known for their Christmas window displays. Walking along the Champs-Élysées after dinner is an easy plan for an evening between sessions.

Christmas Markets

  • Notre-Dame Christmas Market – Square René Viviani, 2 rue du Fouarre (5th arrondissement), Thursday 26 November – Sunday 27 December 2026. Open 10 am – 8 pm (until 9 pm on Fridays and Saturdays). This is the closest market to the Jussieu campus, within walking distance.
  • Paris La Défense Christmas Market – Parvis de La Défense, Thursday 12 November – Thursday 31 December 2026. Open from 11 am (10 am on Saturdays) until 9 pm, and until 11 pm on Fridays and Saturdays.

The Tuileries Garden market is expected to run from November to January as in previous years, but its exact 2026 dates had not been announced at the time of writing (October 2026). The same applies to the Montmartre and Saint-Germain-des-Prés markets.

Museums & Exhibitions

Major exhibitions open throughout the week of the workshop:

  • Musée de l'Orangerie – Monet, peindre le temps (Monet, Painting Time), until 25 January 2027.
  • Louvre – Zurbarán, 7 October 2026 – 25 January 2027.
  • Musée national Picasso-Paris – Kurt Schwitters. Le chaos-monde. 1918–1948, 6 October 2026 – 7 February 2027.
  • Bourse de Commerce – Pinault Collection – Remember Me, a photography exhibition from the Pinault Collection, from 7 October 2026.
  • BnF Richelieu – Louise Bourgeois. Extrême tension (drawings and prints), 20 October 2026 – 21 February 2027.
  • Cité des Sciences et de l'Industrie – Magie (Magic), opening 13 October 2026.

Close to the venue, the Musée de Cluny (medieval art), the Panthéon, the Jardin des Plantes and the Institut du Monde Arabe are all within walking distance of Jussieu.

Musée d'Orsay: 40th Anniversary

The Musée d'Orsay is celebrating its 40th anniversary, with festivities running from 19 November to 12 December 2026. They include a free, family-friendly weekend on 5–6 December, just before the workshop, and the closing party BOUM 1986, directed by Thomas Jolly, on Saturday 12 December from 7 pm in the museum's nave. Admission to the party is free but limited to 1,500 places, by advance reservation on the museum's website.

Shows

Les Misérables is on at the Théâtre du Châtelet from 11 November 2026 to 10 January 2027. Paris also offers concerts and theatre every evening; the city's official tourism website lists current events.

Information checked in October 2026. Dates and opening times may change: please check the official websites before planning your visit.
