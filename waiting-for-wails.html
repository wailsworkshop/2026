Waiting for WAILS

Before we meet in Paris on 9–11 December 2026, join us online for Waiting for WAILS, a series of webinars leading up to the third edition of the Workshop on Artificial Intelligence with and for Learning Sciences. The webinars offer an opportunity to exchange ideas and start conversations around this year’s theme: the responsible and inclusive use of AI in education. We look forward to bringing the WAILS community together before the workshop begins.

How to Participate

Participation is free. To attend a webinar, please register using the link provided below the relevant event.
