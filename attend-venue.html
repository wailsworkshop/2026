How to Get to the Room

  1. Take Metro line 7 or 10 to Jussieu station and come out on place Jussieu.
  2. Enter the campus through the main entrance on place Jussieu (the entrance on the rue Jussieu side, marked Entrée on the map).
  3. Once on the campus esplanade, turn left and walk along the row of towers closest to rue Jussieu.
  4. Tour 26 is the first tower you reach, at the corner of the campus.
  5. Go up to the 1st floor and follow the signs to Room 105 in the corridor between Tour 25 and Tour 26.

Lunch on Thursday

Lunch on Thursday 10 December is served at the TIPI – Maison pour la Science, in Patio 32–43 on the esplanade level (no. 22 on the campus map), a few minutes' walk from Tour 26 in the centre of the campus.

Accessibility

The main entrance on place Jussieu is accessible to people with reduced mobility.

Getting to Paris and to the campus by public transport is described on the Travel Information page.
