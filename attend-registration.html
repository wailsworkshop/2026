Author Registration Policy

Each accepted paper must be accompanied by an Early Bird Full In-person Registration (Regular or PhD Student). A single registration covers up to one accepted paper. Each accepted paper must be presented in person to be included in the proceedings, published by Springer LNCS and available via SpringerLink.

Early Bird Full In-person Registration

Required for paper authors. A full Early Bird registration is mandatory for paper publication in the Springer LNCS proceedings and for in-person presentation.

Online Registration

Available at any time, including before the Standard Registration deadline. After 9 November 2026, only online registrations will be accepted.

Requests for online paper presentations will be considered on a case-by-case basis by the organizing committee. However, paper publication in the proceedings requires a full Early Bird registration — online registration does not qualify.

What Is Included

In-Person Registration (Regular & PhD Student)

  • Attendance at all workshop sessions for three days, including keynote talks
  • Welcome kit provided at the registration desk
  • Coffee breaks throughout the workshop
  • Lunch on the second day
  • Guided tours of Paris organized by the workshop
  • Access to the workshop post-proceedings via SpringerLink

Online Registration

  • Remote attendance at all workshop sessions for three days, including keynote talks
  • Access to the workshop post-proceedings via SpringerLink
  • Special online welcome kit

Cancellation Policy

No refunds are allowed. Registrations cannot be cancelled; the only permitted exception is the transfer of a registration to another person in case of visa denial. Registrations linked to papers in the proceedings cannot be refunded under any circumstances.
